Purpose: keep the four pages (Home, Features, Pricing, Team) looking the same.
All pages share one CSS file (style.css) and one JavaScript file (script.js).

------------------------------------------------------------
1. BRAND
------------------------------------------------------------
Product: BookLoop, a student-to-student platform to buy, sell and swap
university textbooks (uOttawa).
Tagline: "Pass it. Read it. Repeat it."
Look: clean, light and friendly. White pages, lots of space, purple as
the main accent. The blue-to-purple gradient comes from the logo.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. COLORS
------------------------------------------------------------
Navy      #1E2A5E   Main text, headings, footer background
Blue      #2F6FD6   Links and small accents
Purple    #7B2FBE   Main buttons, active menu item, highlights
White     #FFFFFF   Page background, text on purple and navy
Lavender  #F6F5FB   Card background, alternating sections
Gray      #6B7280   Secondary text (captions, small notes)

Gradient: linear-gradient(135deg, #2F6FD6, #7B2FBE)
Use the gradient on one or two elements per page only (main call-to-action
button). Never behind long text.

Rules:
- White and navy dominate. Purple is the accent.
- Text must have a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 with its background.
  Navy on white is about 13:1. White on purple is about 7:1. White on blue
  is about 4.8:1. Gray on white is about 4.8:1.
- Do not use color alone to show meaning (add text or an icon).

------------------------------------------------------------
3. TYPOGRAPHY
------------------------------------------------------------
Font: Poppins (Google Fonts), fallback: Arial, sans-serif.
One font family on the whole site.

Sizes (desktop):
- Page title (h1): 40 px, bold, navy
- Section title (h2): 28 px, semi-bold, navy
- Card title (h3): 20 px, semi-bold, navy
- Body text: 16 px, regular, line height 1.6
- Small text and captions: 14 px, gray

Lines of text should not be wider than about 70 characters.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. LAYOUT AND SPACING
------------------------------------------------------------
- Page content max width: 1100 px, centered.
- Spacing scale: 8, 16, 24, 32, 48 px.
- Space between sections: 48 px.
- Corner radius: 12 px on cards, 999 px (pill) on buttons and search bar.
- Cards: lavender background, 1 px light border, soft shadow.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. HEADER AND MENU (same on all pages)
------------------------------------------------------------
- White background, logo on the left.
- Menu on the right: Features, Pricing, Team, then a Login button.
- Menu links are navy. The link of the current page is purple and
  underlined.
- Hover: link turns purple.
- Clicking the logo goes to Home.html.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. BUTTONS
------------------------------------------------------------
- Main button: purple background (or the gradient), white text,
  pill shape, 12 px by 24 px padding.
- Secondary button (Login): white background, purple outline and text.
- Hover: slightly darker purple. Keyboard focus: visible 2 px outline.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. PAGES
------------------------------------------------------------
HOME
- Full-width background image behind the hero (same image as the Team page),
  with a white or light overlay so the text stays readable.
- Centered title "BookLoop", the tagline, and the search bar
  ("Search for a book") with a search icon.
- A small team icon at the bottom right goes to Team.html.

FEATURES
- Intro: title "Your textbooks, on repeat." and one sentence.
- Four feature cards in a 2 x 2 grid: Buy & Sell, Swap Books,
  Find Your Course, Connect. Each has an icon, a short text and a link.
- "Why BookLoop" list, then a "See pricing" button.

PRICING
- Intro with the title "Simple pricing for students".
- Two cards side by side: Browse & Swap (Free) and Sell a Book (5% per
  completed sale).
- Example box: selling a $60.00 textbook, earnings $57.00, fee $3.00.

TEAM
- Same menu and same background image as Home.
- One rectangle (card) per member, in a row: round profile photo, full
  name, short description, LinkedIn and Gmail links.
- Hover: each rectangle grows (scale 1.05) with a smooth 0.2 s transition.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. IMAGES
------------------------------------------------------------
- Logo: PNG or SVG with a transparent background, about 40 px tall in the
  header.
- Member photos: square JPG, at least 300 x 300 px, shown as a circle.
- Every image has an alt text.
- Files are in the folder "images".

------------------------------------------------------------
9. ACCESSIBILITY
------------------------------------------------------------
- One h1 per page, headings in order (h1, h2, h3).
- Every form field has a label.
- All links and buttons work with the keyboard and show a focus outline.
- Link text says where it goes ("See pricing", not "click here").

------------------------------------------------------------
10. RESPONSIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
- Mobile first. Under 700 px wide, grids become one column and the menu
  wraps under the logo.
- Images never go wider than their container.

------------------------------------------------------------
11. FILES
------------------------------------------------------------

style.css       (all CSS, colors defined once as variables)
script.js       (search bar check)
images/         (logo and member photos)
